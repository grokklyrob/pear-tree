Confessions · Book II

The Pear Tree

Thagaste, around 370 · Confessions II

I loved the evil in me

Alone I would not have done it

Sixteen-year-old Augustine and a pack of friends crept to a neighbor’s pear tree after dark. They shook down far more fruit than they could carry or want. No one was hungry. They stole for the pleasure of the theft itself, then flung the pears to pigs at the orchard edge.

They had better pears at home. Most of the stolen fruit went to the pigs.
